# Spense for Android

A privacy-first expense manager and bill splitter. Spense reads your bank SMS, you approve each entry with one tap, and your money data never leaves your phone: no account, no ads, no cloud AI.

**[⬇ Download the latest Spense (APK)](https://github.com/vikara007/spense-releases/releases/latest/download/spense.apk)** · [All versions](https://github.com/vikara007/spense-releases/releases)

Spense is in early testing and isn't on Google Play yet. This page has only the app, not its source code.

## Install
1. Tap the download link above on your Android phone (Android 9 or newer).
2. Open the downloaded `spense.apk`. If Android asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file app.
3. **If Play Protect says the app was blocked:** in India, Google blocks apps from outside Play that read SMS, because scammers misuse that permission. Spense needs it to read bank SMS on your phone. To install anyway:
   1. Open the **Play Store** → your profile picture → **Play Protect** → ⚙ Settings.
   2. Turn off **Scan apps with Play Protect**.
   3. Install `spense.apk`.
   4. **Turn Play Protect back on** right away.

   Only do this for apps from people you trust. Once Spense is on Google Play, this step goes away.
4. Open Spense and follow the setup. It downloads its on-phone AI model once (about 2 GB; Wi-Fi recommended).
5. Got a group invite link? Tap it again after setup to join the group.

**Motorola phones** may warn "Potentially risky website" when you open an invite link. That's Motorola's own link checker being cautious about a new site. To stop it: Settings → Security Hub → Phishing detection → Allow list → add `spense-ff4ba.web.app`.

## Update
Download the latest APK from the link above and install it over the old one. Your data stays.

## Privacy in short
- Your SMS, entries, amounts, merchants and places stay on your phone, encrypted.
- Shared split groups sync end-to-end encrypted: the relay can't read them.
- Only what you choose to send (📤 feedback, or the opt-in weekly usage counts) ever leaves the phone.
